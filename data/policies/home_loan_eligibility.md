# Home Loan Eligibility Policy

Applicants must be between 23 and 65 years of age at the time of application.
Salaried applicants need a minimum monthly income of INR 40,000 and at least 2 years of total work experience.
Self-employed applicants need a minimum business vintage of 5 years and 3 years of ITR filing.

The minimum CIBIL score for approval is 750. Applicants scoring between 700 and 749 go to manual review.
Applicants below 700 are rejected automatically.

The total EMI burden, including the new loan, must not exceed 60 percent of net monthly income.
The loan amount must not exceed 80 percent of the property value.
