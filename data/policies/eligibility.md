# Personal Loan Eligibility Policy

Applicants must be between 21 and 60 years of age at the time of application.
Salaried applicants need a minimum monthly income of INR 25,000 and at least 1 year of total work experience.
Self-employed applicants need a minimum business vintage of 3 years and 2 years of ITR filing.

The minimum CIBIL score for approval is 700. Applicants scoring between 650 and 699 go to manual review.
Applicants below 650 are rejected automatically.

The total EMI burden, including the new loan, must not exceed 50 percent of net monthly income.
