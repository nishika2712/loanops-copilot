# Interest Rate Policy

Personal loans carry a fixed interest rate between 11 percent and 18 percent per annum, based on the CIBIL score of the applicant.
Home loans carry a floating interest rate linked to the repo rate, with a spread of 2.5 percent for applicants scoring 750 or above.
Home loan applicants scoring below 750 pay a spread of 3.25 percent.

Interest is calculated on the reducing balance method for all loans.
