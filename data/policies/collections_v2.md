# Collections and Delinquency Policy (Version 2.0)

Effective 1 January 2026. This version supersedes Version 1.0.

An account becomes delinquent on the first day after a missed EMI due date.
A reminder SMS is sent on day 1. A call is made on day 3.

From day 30 the account is classified as SMA-1 and a notice is issued.
From day 60 the account is classified as SMA-2 and is escalated to the field collections team.
From day 90 the account becomes a Non-Performing Asset (NPA).

A late payment fee of 3 percent per month is charged on the overdue amount.
Customers facing hardship can request restructuring, which needs approval from the regional credit head.
