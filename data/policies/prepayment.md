# Prepayment and Foreclosure Policy

Part prepayment is allowed after 6 EMIs have been paid. The minimum part payment is 3 times the EMI.
Foreclosure is allowed after 12 EMIs.

A foreclosure charge of 4 percent applies on the outstanding principal for loans under 36 months old.
No foreclosure charge applies for floating rate loans taken by individual borrowers.

A foreclosure statement is issued within 3 working days of the request and is valid for 7 days.
