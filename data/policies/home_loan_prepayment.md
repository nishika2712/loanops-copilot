# Home Loan Prepayment and Foreclosure Policy

Part prepayment is allowed at any time after the first EMI. The minimum part payment is 5 times the EMI.
Foreclosure is allowed after 6 EMIs.

Floating rate home loans taken by individual borrowers carry no foreclosure charge.
A foreclosure charge of 2 percent applies on the outstanding principal for fixed rate home loans.

A foreclosure statement is issued within 5 working days of the request and is valid for 15 days.
